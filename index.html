BlindTown

Accessible visual roleplay town. Start Game enables audio, speech and optional iPhone tilt driving.

Touch: drag to walk, tap nearby objects to interact, double-tap for tool actions, two fingers scan, three fingers say location, three-finger hold recalibrates tilt, four fingers enter/exit car. Keyboard: arrows/WASD move, Enter interacts, C car, R recalibrates, I inventory, S settings.
